The goal

A monitor has no pixels. It has samples.

Most ECG AI starts from a picture of the trace. Ours used to, too: every recording was drawn onto a page and a decoder was trained to put the waveform back. That cost a renderer, a throwaway decoder, and up to half a pixel of rounding on every edge.

So we removed the picture. The cordiq encoder reads the raw 12-lead signal, exactly as the monitor records it, and turns it into a compact representation that a language model can reason over. No rendering, no reconstruction, nothing drawn.

The clinical readout

Five measurements, in units a clinician uses.

The encoder is anchored to cardiology, not just to itself. It reports each value in bpm or milliseconds, its own one-sigma error, and the probability that the measurement exists at all. PR, for example, is absent in 10.4% of the corpus because there is no P wave to measure.

The architecture

Gated Convolutional Retention Network.

GCRN is a signal-native encoder built for what a real bedside monitor sees, not for clean scans.

Each lead is centred on its median and divided by a robust amplitude. Change the monitor's gain by any amount and the encoder's main input stays identical; the amplitude a clinician needs is carried separately as 24 side numbers. Invariance by construction, not by hoping training covered it.

The time axis comes down by reshaping samples into channels, never by striding over them, so a four-sample QRS upstroke is seen in full. One heartbeat at 60 bpm spans 25 tokens, which means morphology lives across the sequence, where the retention branch can follow it.

Baseline wander, mains hum, muscle noise, electrode motion, a dead lead, a railed amplifier, pacing spikes, telemetry dropout, swapped limb leads and a coarse ADC. Every token from the corrupted view must land on the clean view's token at the same instant, so the model learns to separate physiology from artifact.

A new encoder never replaces the current one just because training finished. It is benchmarked on clean and degraded signals against the model on disk, and promoted only if it wins. Robustness that costs the clean case does not count.
